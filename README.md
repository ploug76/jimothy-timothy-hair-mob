# Jimothy Timothy and the Hair Mob

Word on the street, they call him J.T.! Pull hair heists on a schedule with Noodles the ferret, drive the solar Jeep, and win 5 silly showdowns. It's not stealing if it's in the trash. A free game from Peter's Arcade.

Play: https://ploug76.github.io/jimothy-timothy-hair-mob/

Controls: swipe and hold anywhere to move, swipe up or JUMP to jump, ACTION to send Noodles into a bin, honk, or do a showdown move. In the Jeep, swipe left/right to steer. Controllers: stick/D-pad move, A jump, X/B action, Start pause. Keyboard: arrows/WASD, Space jump, X action, P pause, M mute.

For parents: this is a silly cartoon. In real life, never climb in or dig through bins, and ask a grown-up first.

Story: Peter and the Kids story team. Made for Peter's Arcade.
